PADEL OS privacy

Last updated 7 November 2026

The short version

Your workouts and padel stay on your phone. If you make an account, a copy is saved to it so a new phone can get everything back. Only you can see that copy. Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads.

Without an account

Everything is stored only on your phone, inside the app. Nothing is sent anywhere.

With an account

  • Signing in: your email address. If you continue with Google, Google tells us your email address and name; we don't get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.
  • What's saved: the same data as a backup file: your profile, settings, workouts, padel sessions and scores. It's saved to your account after each change while the app is open, plus one earlier copy per hour of use (the last 30).
  • Who can see it: only you. The database refuses anyone who isn't signed in as you.
  • Where it's kept: with Supabase (supabase.com), the service that runs the accounts, on servers in Tokyo.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your account → Delete Account removes your account and every saved copy straight away. The data on your phone stays until you delete the app.
